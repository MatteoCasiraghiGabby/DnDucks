About the platform

A modern command center for Dungeon Masters.

DnDucks is being built as a focused workspace where Dungeon Masters can keep campaigns, sessions, worldbuilding, homebrew, characters, maps, files, and future AI insights in one place.

CoreNotes

Campaign notes

Capture session recaps, lore, secrets, quests, locations, and unresolved consequences before they vanish between sessions.

CoreAtlas

Worldbuilding

Organize factions, locations, timelines, important NPC relationships, and the evolving history of your setting.

ToolsHomebrew

Homebrew creation

Draft magic items, spells, rules, monsters, and equipment with searchable categories and eventual campaign-specific databases.

DM-onlyCast

Characters and sheets

Track NPCs now, then expand into complete character sheets, player handouts, and permissions for shared campaigns.

PreparedMaps

Maps and files

Prepare for map uploads, image pins, PDF references, player handouts, and attachments tied directly to campaign documents.

FutureAI

AI assistant

The long-term assistant will link events, detect contradictions, summarize sessions, and suggest consequences from approved campaign context.

Where this prototype fits

Phase 1 is intentionally static: plain HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and localStorage. That keeps the prototype fast, inspectable, and usable while the product shape is still forming. Later phases can add accounts, a database, uploads, a rich editor, embeddings, retrieval-augmented AI, maps, and collaboration.

The future account system should let each user sign up, log in, create private campaigns, upload documents, and choose which campaign materials the AI assistant is allowed to read.

DnDucks keeps campaign lore linked, session prep visible, and live-table tools close.
